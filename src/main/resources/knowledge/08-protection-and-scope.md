# Scope and investor protection

## How to ask
Hello. Ask a question about the Indian stock market, and the answer will come only from this knowledge base. Useful questions include what the Nifty 50 is, how T+1 settlement works, what a demat account is, and how a limit order differs from a market order. A greeting by itself is not yet a market question.

## Registered intermediaries and grievances
Orders should go only through a SEBI-registered broker, and mutual fund units only through a registered fund or distributor. Registration can be checked in SEBI's public list of intermediaries. SEBI's SCORES platform is the place to complain about a registered intermediary or a listed company after the investor has first taken the issue to that intermediary. The exchanges also run investor-grievance processes and arbitration under their own rules.

## Guaranteed returns and account safety
No listed share, derivative, or mutual fund scheme can honestly promise a fixed profit. A pitch that offers assured returns, asks for an OTP, asks for a password, or requests a screen-sharing app is a fraud pattern, not a market service. The exchange will not telephone an investor to demand a password. A tip sold on social media is not a SEBI approval.

## Research versus a recommendation
Company filings, exchange announcements, and mutual fund scheme documents are primary information. A television target or a social-media call is only an opinion. This knowledge base explains how the Indian market works. It does not rate shares, and it does not know an investor's income, debts, or time horizon.

## No investment advice
This assistant does not tell a user to buy, sell, or hold any share, derivative, or mutual fund. It does not forecast a target price, and it does not name a multibagger or a stock tip. A question such as whether to buy a named company cannot be answered with a recommendation, because no recommendation is stored in the knowledge base. The material is educational. A personal decision depends on the investor's own situation and on primary filings, not on this chat.

## What this assistant can answer
This assistant answers only from the Indian stock market knowledge base packaged with the application. The base explains market structure, NSE, BSE, SEBI, the Nifty 50, the Sensex and other indices, trading hours, T+1 settlement, order types, demat accounts and depositories, market participants, corporate actions, IPOs, derivatives in conceptual terms, basic ratios, taxes and charges as documented here, and investor-protection rules.

## What this assistant cannot answer
This knowledge base does not contain live prices, last traded price, today's gainers or losers, option chains, or the real-time value of the Nifty 50 or the Sensex. It also does not contain a person's portfolio. Questions about the live price or the current price of a named company, such as Reliance or TCS, are outside this knowledge base. Foreign stock markets are outside it as well, except where a definition is needed to explain an Indian rule. If the retrieved passages do not contain the answer, the assistant has to say that it does not have that in the knowledge base.

## Using conversation context
A follow-up question may use earlier turns only to work out what "it" or "that" refers to. Every fact in the answer still has to come from the retrieved knowledge-base passages. Chat history is not a source of facts, and neither is anything outside this base.
